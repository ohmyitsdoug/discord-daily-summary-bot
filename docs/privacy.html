Privacy Policy

What the bot keeps, and for how long

Daily Games Bot (“the bot”) is a Discord bot that reads daily puzzle results people paste into a channel and turns them into leaderboards. This policy explains what it collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Discord user ID and display name, when you post a recognized game result.
  • The score details from that result (game name, puzzle number, score) plus the server channel ID, message ID, and the date it was posted.
  • For games added with /daily-games-summary addgame: the game name, the text pattern used to recognize it, scoring settings, and the user ID of the person who added it.

What we don't collect

  • The bot reads messages only in the one channel a server manager chooses for it, in order to spot game results, but it does not store the text of any message. Messages that aren't recognized game results are ignored.
  • No email addresses, IP addresses, payment details, voice data, or direct messages.

How it's used

Only to show leaderboards, daily recaps, and weekly standings in the same channel where the scores were posted. Data from one channel is never shown in another channel or server. We don't sell, rent, or share this data, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Scores are deleted automatically after 7 days. Added games stay until someone removes them with /daily-games-summary removegame or the bot is removed from the server.

Where it's stored

In a database on a server hosted by Fly.io in the United States. Discord also processes messages under its own Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Don't want your scores tracked? Don't post results in channels where the bot is active, or delete your post.
  • Server admins can remove the bot or limit which channels it can see at any time.
  • To ask for your data to be deleted sooner, contact us through playsmidgen.com with your Discord username.

Children

The bot is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
